I have set it up as a [Proxmox](https://proxmox.com/en/) server. I have the OS installed on a 512GB NVMe SSD and have 2 1TB NVMe SSDs setup as a mirror for VM storage. I have migrated a setup that was previously on a Dell OptiPlex to an Ubuntu VM running on the new server. It is working great and I have a ton of overhead to add more VMs and services.
