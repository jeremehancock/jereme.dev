I have been wanting to track more data related to the Pi Lab nodes. After a lot of research I decided to use [Prometheus](https://prometheus.io/) and [Grafana](https://grafana.com/). 

The results are really awesome!

I have updated the [Pi Lab Stats](/pi-lab-stats) page to incorporate these dashboards. 

If you are interested in how I set this up check out this GitHub repo.
https://github.com/bhemar/raspberry-metrics.
