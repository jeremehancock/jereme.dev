Imagine a world where your media library looks as good as it feels. Posteria is the solution media enthusiasts have been waiting for – a sleek, intuitive platform that transforms how you organize and showcase your movie, TV show, and collection artwork.

## Why Posteria Matters

For anyone with a growing media collection, poster management can quickly become a frustrating mess. Mismatched artwork, cluttered folders, and inconsistent designs can turn your digital library into a visual nightmare. Posteria changes all that.

### Features That Make a Difference

<span class="emoji">🖼️</span> **Beautiful, Intuitive Design**
Say goodbye to cluttered poster libraries. Posteria offers a clean, modern interface that makes managing your media artwork as enjoyable as watching your favorite shows.

<span class="emoji">📁</span> **Comprehensive Organization**
Keep everything perfectly sorted with dedicated categories for:
- Movies
- TV Shows
- TV Seasons
- Collections

<span class="emoji">🔍</span> **Lightning-Fast Search**
No more endless scrolling. The powerful fuzzy search lets you find exactly the poster you want in seconds.

<span class="emoji">📱</span> **Access Anywhere, Anytime**
Fully mobile-responsive and installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA), Posteria goes wherever you go.

<span class="emoji">⚡</span> **Effortless Poster Management**
- Upload from Local files
- Upload from Direct URLs
- Import from your Plex Media Server
- Easily move between categories

<span class="emoji">🔒</span> **Simple, Secure Access**
Protect your carefully curated collection with an easy-to-use authentication system.
