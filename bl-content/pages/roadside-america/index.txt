A little over a year ago I saw a Reddit post about the [John Margolies Roadside America Photograph Archive](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/john-margolies-roadside-america-photograph-archive). The photos were taken between 1968 and 2008 and feature various roadside attractions in the United States. 

My first thought was I should build some sort of API to allow someone to easily use these photos on a website. So I built out a very simple API of sorts and set it up here, [Roadside America API](https://roadside-america.dumbprojects.com/).

I started tweeting the pictures on my personal Twitter account and people seemed to like them. So I have setup a Roadside America Twitter account and new site to allow people to get a random picture from the archive wiith its description every hour.
