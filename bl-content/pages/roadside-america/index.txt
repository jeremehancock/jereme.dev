Several years ago, I discovered the **John Margolies Roadside America Photograph Archive** from the Library of Congress — a collection of roadside attractions photographed between 1968 and 2008.

I wanted an easy way to use these images on websites, so I built a simple API that returns a random photo from the archive along with its description.

I also created a demo site that shows a random image from the collection.

It started as a small experiment — making historic archival photos easier to explore and reuse.
