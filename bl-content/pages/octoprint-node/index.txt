My wife and kids got me a new 3D printer for my Birthday! I have been wanting one for quite some time. It is an [Ender 3 Pro](https://creality3d.shop/collections/3d-printer/products/creality3d-ender-3-pro-high-precision-3d-printer). I am really just getting into using it but one of the first things I was told to do was check out [OctoPrint](https://octoprint.org/). 

> So what is OctoPrint?

It is described as *"The snappy web interface for your 3D printer."*.  That doesn't begin to explain how great I think it is. It definitely does provide a web interface for your 3D printer, but it does so much more. It also provides the ability to run manual commands and even select from a very nice selection of plugins for a variety of tasks. It truly made a nice 3D printer a much more enjoyable piece of equipment.

I have re-purposed my [Monitor Node](pi-lab-monitor-node) into my new OctoPi Node.

So I went looking on [Thingverse](https://www.thingiverse.com) and I found this [Bumper Extension for Roomba](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1756175).
