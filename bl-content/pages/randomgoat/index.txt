**Random Goat** is a simple website that shows random goat GIFs.

That’s it — just goats.

Click or tap to see a new goat anytime. No ads, no tracking, no sign-ups. Just fun, fast goat content designed to make you smile.

---

### Features

- 🎲 Endless random goat GIFs  
- 📱 Works on all devices  
- ⚡ Fast and lightweight  
- 🎃 Seasonal holiday goats  
- 🔗 Easy to embed  

---

**In a chaotic world, be someone’s random goat. 🐐**
