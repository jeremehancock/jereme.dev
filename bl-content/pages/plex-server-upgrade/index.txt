The base is a smaller one so I knew I had to check out the BX. I have always found great deals on returned items at the BX on small bases.

I was shocked to find something better than a returned item. It was a display model. It was a [Aspire TC-1750-UR11](https://store.acer.com/en-us/aspire-tc-desktop-tc-1750-ur11) which has an i5-12400 Intel CPU with QuickSync. This would be a nice upgrade from my Dell OptiPlex 3060 with an i3-8100. The price was the best part.** $150!** 

That's right I got it for $150 with no sales tax since it was at the BX.

I was able to migrate my existing Plex install to a 1TB NVMe drive that I already had. I put that drive in the new machine and it just fired right up. I also upgraded the RAM with 2 16GB sticks that I already had on hand.

It has been an awesome upgrade and the migration was super simple!
