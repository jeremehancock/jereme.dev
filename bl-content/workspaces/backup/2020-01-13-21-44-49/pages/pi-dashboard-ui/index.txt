If you are looking for an easy way to display your Raspberry Pi's metrics in real time look no further. 

The installation couldn't be simpler. If you already have a webserver and PHP configured on your Raspberry Pi you simply have to put the files in a sub-directory of your website and it just works!

I have installed it on the 5 production nodes of Pi Lab. Check it out here: <a href="https://pilab.dev/Node-1" target="_blank">pilab.dev/Node-1</a>

The project is available on GitHub and is definitely worth a look.
