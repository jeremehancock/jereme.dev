*Pi Lab is a collection of Raspberry Pis setup for various purposes throughout my home. *

## Pi Lab Web Cluster:

*   **Load Balancer**
    *   1 Raspberry Pi 3 Model B
				* [Samsung EVO Plus 32GB](https://www.amazon.com/Samsung-Micro-Memory-Adapter-MB-MC64GA/dp/B073S49S8M) SD Card
*   **Dev/Prod Node**
    *    1 Raspberry Pi 2 Model B
				* [Samsung EVO Plus 32GB](https://www.amazon.com/Samsung-Micro-Memory-Adapter-MB-MC64GA/dp/B073S49S8M) SD Card
*  **Prod Nodes**
    *    2 Raspberry Pi 2 Model B
				* [Samsung EVO Plus 32GB](https://www.amazon.com/Samsung-Micro-Memory-Adapter-MB-MC64GA/dp/B073S49S8M) SD Cards
    *    2 Raspberry Pi 4 4GB Model Bs 
						* [Samsung EVO Plus 32GB](https://www.amazon.com/Samsung-Micro-Memory-Adapter-MB-MC64GA/dp/B073S49S8M) SD Cards
*    **Operating System:** [Raspbian](https://www.raspbian.org/)
*    **Webserver/Load Balancer:** [Nginx](https://nginx.org/)
*    **SSL:** [Let's Encrypt](https://letsencrypt.org/)
*   **CMS:** [Bludit - v3.10.0](https://www.bludit.com/) PRO
*    **Theme:** [Blekathlon](https://blthemes.pp.ua/) (Pi Lab Version)
*    **Plugins:** [Pi Lab Plugins](plugins)
*    **Network** 
    *    [Linksys WRT1900AC](https://www.linksys.com/us/support-product?pid=01t80000003KdJ7AAK) - Bridge Mode
    *    [Netgear ProSAFE GS105](https://www.netgear.com/support/product/GS105v5)
*    **Power:** [CyberPower EC850LCD](https://www.cyberpowersystems.com/product/ups/ec850lcd/)
*    **Cooling:** [AC Infinity MULTIFAN S4](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00MWH4FL4)
